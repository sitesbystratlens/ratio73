Ingredients & formula

The Season 7 formula includes centella-derived ingredients, niacinamide, panthenol, ceramide NP and retinol. It also contains fragrant essential oils.

Panthenol + ceramide NP
Common ingredients in moisturising and barrier-care formulas.
Retinol
Listed in the formula; check your routine if you are sensitive to retinoids.

Contains retinol and fragrant essential oils. Retinoid skincare is generally avoided during pregnancy; ask a healthcare professional if unsure. Always check the ingredient list on your pack.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few things to know

What is ratio73?

ratio73 is a Korean skincare retailer concept. We plan to sell products from established brands, beginning with Centellian24. We don’t manufacture the products.

Where will ratio73 deliver?

We plan to launch in Australia and the United States. We’ll confirm delivery areas and shipping costs before orders open.

Why only one product?

We’re starting with Centellian24 Madeca Cream Time Reverse. As the range expands, we’re aiming for 70% established favourites and 30% new or trending skincare products.

Can I place an order?

Not yet. This is a preview site. Prices are examples, and the bag is for demonstration only. Orders, payments and email signup are not enabled.

What is Club 73?

Club 73 is a community we’re planning for Korean skincare lovers. We hope to share new discoveries, welcome recommendations and offer member benefits as we grow. Your feedback may help inform future product choices, but membership and participation features aren’t live yet.
